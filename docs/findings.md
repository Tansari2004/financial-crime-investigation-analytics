## What an analyst might investigate

ACH has 4,483 labelled transactions out of 600,797 (0.7462%). This is a pattern in this synthetic dataset, not evidence that ACH payments in real banks have the same risk.

Bank 070 / account 100428660 has 2,829 outgoing payments in one calendar hour. That is high activity, but context such as an institutional account could explain it. Activity alone does not establish laundering.

One USD payment of 1,343,955,575.58 is about 14.74 million times its sender's qualifying prior average of approximately USD 91.17, based on 15 earlier payments. Extreme ratios warrant investigation of simulation artifacts and sparse history as well as business context. No predictive success is claimed from this example.

## Measurement limits

The SQL quality checks do not test every possible source issue. In particular, the NUMERIC(18,2) import can round source values with greater precision; Bitcoin is present. Source precision should be audited before modelling or asset-level financial analysis. Totals here describe the stored representation.

Cross-currency averages are intentionally omitted. Summing outgoing payments can count the same money as it moves repeatedly, so transaction totals must not be described as losses or unique exposure.

The V1 deliverable is descriptive SQL analysis. No risk model, alert-performance evaluation, dashboard, or real-world impact has been measured.
